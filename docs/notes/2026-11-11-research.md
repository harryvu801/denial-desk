## 3. Stage 2 open decisions (DECISIONS.md "still open")

### Corpus sources (public, four payers)

- UT: Utah Medicaid Provider Manual, Section I plus the HCBS/home health program manuals at
  medicaid.utah.gov (PDF; Section I alone is 139 pages).
- AZ: AHCCCS FFS Provider Billing Manual, per-chapter PDFs (Ch. 4 billing rules, Ch. 28
  disputes, plus the HCBS chapters) at azahcccs.gov; AHCCCS Medical Policy Manual (AMPM).
- NV: Billing Manual (49 pages) at medicaid.nv.gov; Medicaid Services Manual chapters at
  dhcfp.nv.gov (MSM Ch. 100 Medicaid Program, Ch. 2200 Personal Care Services, Ch. 3300
  Program Integrity).
- ID: Provider Handbook, General Billing Instructions (157 pages) and General Information
  and Requirements for Providers at idmedicaid.com.
- All four extract cleanly with pypdf (checked on the five PDFs above). All are numbered
  policy sections, which is the premise of ADR-008's section-aware chunker.

### Embedding model (options, with a recommendation)

| Option | Cost | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Voyage `voyage-3-large` / `voyage-3.5`** (hosted) | ~$0.06 per 1M tokens | Strongest retrieval quality in current comparisons; Anthropic-adjacent; cheap at this corpus size (a few hundred pages is well under $1) | Second vendor and key; recordings would need the same record/replay discipline (ADR-030) for the eval to run keyless |
| **OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small`** (hosted) | $0.02 per 1M tokens | Cheapest hosted, widest tooling | Third vendor; weaker than Voyage on retrieval benchmarks |
| **BAAI `bge-m3`** (local, open weights) | $0 | No key, fully reproducible, supports dense + sparse in one model (useful for the hybrid comparison); runs on CPU for a corpus this size | Slower indexing on a laptop; ~2 GB download; lower MTEB than the hosted options |

Recommendation: **bge-m3 locally** as the default, because the whole repo's promise is
"reproduces with no API key" and a hosted embedder breaks that unless embeddings are also
recorded. Keep Voyage as the measured comparison once the retrieval golden set exists; the
comparison is itself a deliverable (ADR-008's spirit). This is the author's decision and
wants an ADR either way.

### Reranker

| Option | Cost | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **`BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3`** (local cross-encoder) | $0 | Open default in current comparisons; ~250 ms for 20 pairs on CPU, fine at k=20 |
| Cohere Rerank | ~$2 per 1k searches | Highest quality in current comparisons; third vendor |
| Voyage `rerank-2.5` | ~$0.05 per 1M tokens | Cheaper than Cohere; pairs with Voyage embeddings |

Recommendation: local bge-reranker-v2-m3 for the same keyless-reproducibility reason, with
a hosted reranker as the measured comparison if latency or quality is short. The refusal
floor (ADR-011, `retrieval_min_score`) is calibrated on the reranker's scores, so the choice
fixes the floor's scale.
